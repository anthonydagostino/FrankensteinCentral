# Standing Product Vision

Owner: Codex, Product Owner
Source: Anthony's explicit product direction, 2026-09-06
Status: standing roadmap direction; current task authorization remains in STATE.json and PRODUCT_DIRECTIVE.md

## Purpose

Make FrankensteinCentral Anthony's essential daily personal information hub: the first useful place to look, because it makes life easier and simpler. Connect information across his existing services and explain what it means together, rather than merely displaying separate app counters.

## Standing mandate

Anthony authorizes Codex to continuously maintain and prioritize the backlog, identify useful improvements, issue bounded directives to Claude, independently review evidence, request corrections, and authorize routine releases through the deterministic release service. Do not wait for Anthony to specify every feature. Claude implements only the current directive and does not choose the roadmap or accept its own work.

This is not authorization for unlimited spend, new credentials or account access, destructive personal-data changes, or weakened security boundaries. Do not send external messages on Anthony's behalf without explicit authorization. Routine decisions and coordination belong to Codex.

## Daily experience

Within one minute of opening the hub, Anthony should understand:
- What needs attention today, and why?
- What changed since the last visit?
- What commitments, deadlines or conflicts are approaching?
- What is the next useful action, with the relevant context already assembled?

Aim to reduce forgotten obligations, repetitive checking, unnecessary app switching and manual entry. Earn repeat use through usefulness and trust. Avoid engagement tricks, noisy notifications, or adding panels simply to increase feature count.

## Product principles

1. Trust comes first: source links, freshness timestamps and honest missing/disconnected/error states. Never present stale or unknown data as a current fact.
2. Connect context: relate relevant information across authorized sources instead of leaving isolated widgets.
3. Make information actionable: prioritize, explain and provide a short path to the next step. Begin with read-only context and explicit actions; consequential external actions need their own authorization.
4. Keep the default experience calm, fast and usable on phone and desktop. Reveal depth when it is useful.
5. Minimize maintenance for Anthony. Agents own routine diagnosis, tests, review, deployment verification and corrections.
6. Preserve privacy. This repository is public: store only generic requirements and synthetic examples here, never actual account data, messages, financial details or credentials.
7. Improve what evidence shows is useful; simplify or remove weak features rather than accumulating clutter.

## Initial priority order

0. Finish the autonomous-loop bootstrap and resolve the existing release-review blockers. No feature initiative interrupts FC-002.
1. Audit the actual dashboard and integration health; identify the most costly daily friction using available evidence. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses.
2. Build a focused Today experience from existing authorized data: a concise daily brief, upcoming commitments and items needing attention, with provenance and freshness.
3. Connect related items across inbox, calendar, tasks and other already connected sources; improve context, deduplication and navigation.
4. Add useful cross-source search and change detection as justified by the audit.
5. Expand integrations or proactive delivery only when they materially improve daily use and required access/consent exists.

These are candidate roadmap outcomes for Codex to refine into testable directives, not simultaneous implementation authorization.

## How progress is judged

Define a baseline and acceptance evidence for each initiative: time to understand the day, steps needed for a common action, reliability and freshness, duplicated entry eliminated, relevance of surfaced items, and Anthony's feedback. Prefer demonstrated time saved and obligations surfaced over raw feature count, screen time or notification volume. Do not claim observed usage or improvement without evidence, and do not add third-party tracking by default.

## Operating cadence

Codex's recurring task checks progress every 15 minutes when scheduling is available. Work is sequential: finish or explicitly resolve the current task, verify its release outcome, then select the next highest-value bounded improvement. Keep a prioritized backlog and durable decision history. Stay quiet on unchanged polls; report meaningful shipped outcomes, material failures, or genuinely necessary user decisions.

Scheduled checks are not a claim that implementation is running continuously or that the unattended loop has already been proven.
