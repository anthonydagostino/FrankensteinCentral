💵 What's remote worth in $? WFH research (Bloom/Barrero/Davis SWAA surveys) puts the average worker's value on remote flexibility at ~7–8% of salary — but that's a population average, not someone who's told me remote is a top-tier priority. Personalized, honest number: ~$25k–$30k/yr for fully remote vs. 5-days-onsite somewhere you wouldn't otherwise choose to live.

Caveat that matters more than the number: you've said you want to move to NYC anyway. AIRIA (Brooklyn) and EliseAI (NYC HQ) are both already in that city — so their in-office days aren't "forced relocation," they're just a normal commute in a place you're choosing to live regardless. The remote-tax barely applies to those two. It's DeepL's "2 days/week in-person" location that's the real open question — if that office isn't in or near NYC, that's worth asking about directly before you assume it fits your relocation plan.
